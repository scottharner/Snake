saturn
------
- why does my tile show garbled when using jo engine and map editor?
	- you cant use compression when saving a graphic as tga
- what is the default resolution with jo engine?
	- 320x240
- what is the difference between jo_sprite_draw3D and jo_sprite_draw3D2?
	- first one uses center aligned coords and the second uses top left coords
- jo_core_init must occur before other jo calls
	- i was mistakenly calling jo_malloc first and then my work was getting clobbered with the subsequent call to jo_core_init
